# Andromeda project mandate

Andromeda exists to discover, validate and eventually exploit a sustainable economic advantage in prediction markets. The operator considers even $5–10 per week of consistent supplemental income worthwhile if credible evidence supports it. Profitability is unproven.

Choose engineering work by the economic research question it unlocks. State the evidence that would falsify each hypothesis, account for uncertainty and costs, and stop weak directions early. Time already invested, dashboard polish and milestone completion do not establish economic value.

Current work is personal, paper-only research. Keep genuine records and credentials out of Git, CI, external AI tools and logs. Publish aggregate diagnostics and test with independent synthetic fixtures. The prospective pilot requires an explicit operator launch.
