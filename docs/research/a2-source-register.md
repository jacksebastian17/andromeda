# A2 source register and bounded inspections

Reviewed **25 November 2026**. The seven entries below identify the publisher, the applicable data terms, and the limits of each proposed use. Prices are published USD amounts on the review date. Only the four sources in the inspection log were sampled. No accounts were created and no third-party raw files were retained or committed. Kalshi authorization and technical documentation are covered in the [decision report](a2-data-feasibility.md).

## Candidate sources

### 1. Good Judgment Project experimental prediction markets

**Best H1 candidate at $0.** IARPA identifies the four-year ACE forecasting tournament as the source. Good Judgment Project deposited *GJP Data*, Harvard Dataverse v1.0, released 11 November 2016. The deposit's native metadata specifies **CC0 1.0** with no additional terms of use or access listed. This permits offline analysis, fitting, storage and redistribution within the dedication's scope. Cite the deposit and analyze question-level prices without participant profiles. This is first-party research material. [Deposit DOI](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/BPCDH5) · [Dataset license and file metadata](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/datasets/:persistentId/?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/BPCDH5) · [IARPA release](https://www.iarpa.gov/newsroom/article/iarpa-announces-publication-of-data-from-the-good-judgment-project)

The deposit covers the 2011–15 tournament. Its prediction-market README distinguishes continuous double auctions in years 2–3 from market-scoring-rule arms in years 3–4. Some year-four arms begin with batch auctions. Condition, question, branch, option, trade ID and transaction time matter. The archive contains human-submitted beliefs as well as market prices. Final answers are option codes tied to question wording. The prefix below establishes two nominally binary links. Eligibility at a particular horizon, time zone, price sequencing, original closing schedules and representativeness remain open. Suitable for experimental-market H1 and selected H2 logic checks. Neither a Kalshi sample nor a sports or weather forecasting benchmark. [Deposit market README](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/2917338)

### 2. Manifold historical markets and bets

Manifold offers dumps for personal, noncommercial analysis, academic research and backtesting. The free page lists markets since December 2021, an 87 MB market dump dated 6 July 2024 and a 967 MB bet dump dated 4 July 2024. Local download for the stated use is explicit. Further raw redistribution and external AI disclosure were not established. The dump page offers commercial/AI training rights by individually priced license. The API guidance permits academic/personal/noncommercial use and requires a commercial AI/ML license. Confirm the applicable channel and whether this project's intended downstream use and model fitting qualify before acquisition. No dump obtained. [Dump terms, sizes and licensing](https://docs.manifold.markets/data) · [API terms and types](https://docs.manifold.markets/api) · [Terms of service](https://docs.manifold.markets/terms)

Market ID, bet ID, contract ID, bet creation time, before/after probabilities, market close, resolution and resolution time could support H1. A resolved record's close can reflect resolution itself. An as-known scheduled close needs separate evidence. Use a verified YES/NO label and exclude cancellations or probability resolutions. Creator adjudication, play-money incentives, correlated questions and changing mechanisms affect generalization. Conditional fallback for H1 and verified H2 relationships. Establish no Kalshi economics, H3 overlap or independent H4 features from these fields.

### 3. FiveThirtyEight published forecast evaluation data

FiveThirtyEight's own evaluation repository publishes project CSVs under CC BY 4.0. Attribution and a license link are required for sharing covered material, with changes identified. Local storage, adaptation and redistribution are available on those terms. This report credits FiveThirtyEight and summarizes selected file prefixes. No source rows are reproduced. It is a static archive, $0, with project-specific dates, participants, probabilities and outcome columns. Different projects use different targets. Keys such as date and team pair or forecast date, player and round must be checked per file. The sampled files do not expose a common vendor game ID or intraday release timestamp. [Pinned publisher repository](https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/checking-our-work-data/tree/f6d5b2e1d6da2889345d381c41431f9a4ee208dd) · [License](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fivethirtyeight/checking-our-work-data/f6d5b2e1d6da2889345d381c41431f9a4ee208dd/LICENSE)

Useful for calibration of **published model forecasts** and H3 target-matching design. These are selected projects, seasons and competitions. Repeated team-round forecasts depend on one tournament. Binary outcome columns reflect the publisher's target, which can differ from a venue's settlement rule. The observations below cannot verify same-hour comparisons, market-price calibration, H2 execution or unrestricted historical sports coverage.

### 4. CollegeBasketballData (CBBD)

Rad Sports Analytics' API terms effective **12 August 2026**, §§2–8, allow private caching, retention after access ends, historical analysis, feature engineering, model training and publication of derived outputs. Public raw feeds, bulk redistribution and third-party access to raw API responses are restricted. Packs have separate licenses. Attribution is appreciated. A verified-email key gets 1,000 free calls/month shared with the football API. Tier 1 is $1/month, Tier 2 $5 and Tier 3 $10. No key or paid access obtained. [Terms](https://collegebasketballdata.com/terms) · [Access tiers](https://www.collegebasketballdata.com/api-tiers)

The provider lists games/scores from 1949 with sparse early seasons, lines from 2013 with gaps, and daily ratings history from 2014 (current season Tier 2, previous seasons Tier 3). Game/team IDs, scheduled start, home/away scores and provider lines support a college H4 baseline and H3 identity checks. The documented lines example includes open spread/total and other line and moneyline fields. It supplies no per-quote issue time. Neither field order nor game start establishes when a line was available. Results are unofficial and revisable. Historical ratings snapshots use a 10:00 UTC game-start inclusion boundary, which does not prove publication at that instant. Cannot support Kalshi calibration, purely logical contract joins, or H3 at a fixed earlier cutoff by itself. [Coverage](https://api.collegebasketballdata.com/data-availability) · [Lines schema](https://api.collegebasketballdata.com/api/lines) · [Update and correction policy](https://www.collegebasketballdata.com/about)

### 5. The Odds API

The provider aggregates bookmaker prices. Terms updated **31 August 2026**, under “Restrictions,” explicitly allow indefinite retention, research, derived values and statistical/ML training. Standalone raw redistribution is prohibited. A key is required. The free 500-credit monthly plan excludes historical odds prices. The first listed historical-price tier is **$30/month for 20,000 credits**. Historical event and market discovery are available on free plans at one credit per request, without odds. No subscription, discovery or price request made. [Terms](https://the-odds-api.com/terms-and-conditions.html) · [Prices](https://the-odds-api.com/) · [Historical access and coverage](https://the-odds-api.com/historical-odds-data/)

Featured markets have snapshots from June 2020 subject to sport and bookmaker start dates. The historical query returns the nearest snapshot at or before the requested time. Save actual snapshot time, bookmaker update time, event ID, scheduled commencement, market, side and odds format. Availability varies by bookmaker, market type and lead time. A same-game score from another source still needs compatible grading rules. Good potential independent H3 comparator if two licensed sides, a verified common population and a future budget exist. Prices alone supply neither final exchange outcomes, house-rule equivalence, depth nor an independent H4 feature archive. [Historical schema and sport-specific dates](https://the-odds-api.com/historical-odds-data/)

### 6. NOAA US Climate Reference Network observations

NOAA/NCEI USCRN Products cover station-dependent periods from November 2000. Use the US NOAA station product under NCEI's open-data policy for federally created data. Store and analyze applicable public-domain US data, cite the source on redistribution and preserve processing/version information. NCEI also holds third-party products with different terms. The daily file keys are station WBAN and local-standard-time date. Hourly and sub-hourly products also exist. Missing sentinels and QC decisions affect target completeness. Updated yearly files incorporate corrections. NCEI identifies a separate updates archive of real-time transmissions. Station-day outcomes support H4 weather if the station and observation window match. They supply no quote, contract, or as-issued forecast. $0 public archive. [NCEI policy §4](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-12/NCEI%20PD-10-2-02%20-%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20Signed.pdf) · [USCRN dataset and citation](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/metadata/geoportal/rest/metadata/item/gov.noaa.ncdc:C00546/html) · [Products and updates](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/crn/products.html) · [Daily format](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/uscrn/products/daily01/README.txt)

### 7. NOAA High-Resolution Rapid Refresh operational forecast archive

NOAA's HRRR is a regional 3 km model. NOAA lists GRIB archives since 2014 and documents operational version changes. For the contiguous-US product, run date/hour, forecast lead, parameter, level and grid location identify a prediction for a valid time. The nominal cycle can precede actual dissemination. Require as-issued files and release-time evidence, check missing cycles, model changes, terrain and station/grid mismatch. NODD allows public use of NOAA data, including storage and redistribution, requests credit, and requires modified products be identified without implying NOAA endorsement. The public GRIB/index path needs no account. Data access $0, with separate compute/storage costs possible at scale. Candidate H4 feature paired with independent station outcomes. A single model run provides neither a calibrated probability nor a settlement label. No standalone H1–H3 test. [NOAA model and archive history](https://rapidrefresh.noaa.gov/hrrr/) · [NODD archive license and access](https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-hrrr-pds/)
