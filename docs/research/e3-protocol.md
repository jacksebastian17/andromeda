# E3 frozen retrospective NBA calibration protocol

Use ordinary KXNBAGAME full-game winners dated October 28 through November 26, 2026, inclusive, in the North American regular season. The calendar follows the NBA's preseason release. Select one participant per game, the lower canonical participant UUID. Choose games and participants independently of direction, status, prices and outcomes. Exclude every NBA event ID found in A3.0 market listings or individual event requests using identifiers alone. Keep E1 parked and defer the weather-window study. [NBA regular-season release](https://pr.nba.com/2026-27-nba-regular-season-schedule)

Freeze development as October 28–November 12, validation as November 13–18, and test as November 19–26. Within each partition sort event IDs by SHA-256 of the full ID and retain up to 108, 40 and 52 respectively before any price or eligibility checks. Do not replace an excluded game. The calendar-window frame uses both the untimed current series listing (maximum 16 pages of 100) and the live close-time listing padded seven days either side (maximum 12 pages of 100). If the archive settlement boundary intersects the padded period, include up to 12 historical pages of 100. Publish cursor, duplication and unresolved diagnostics. Raw successful market bodies can contain labels. Use only identity fields to freeze the private roster. Read label direction only for the applicable analysis stage.

Decision time is **10:00 UTC on the date named in the winner rules**, before ordinary North American NBA tip-offs. Verify dated full-game winner rules, distinct participants, two siblings, a shared date and event identity. Reject ambiguous identity, already closed, not yet open, nonbinary or non-$1 contracts, and any recorded occurrence or settlement at/before decision. Final timestamps serve exclusion and label checks only. This horizon does not establish actual tip-off or original publication. Treat every surviving feature as a retrospective proxy.

Use the latest valid, completed one-minute candle with its entire minute after market open, end no later than decision minus 120 seconds and age at most 60 minutes. The primary probability is the arithmetic midpoint of the selected participant's YES bid and ask closes divided by $1. Require finite decimal sides with 0 < bid ≤ ask < 1. Conflicting duplicate candle ends invalidate that contract. Retain the quoted spread, bar-end age and latest positive-volume trade close under the same clock rules as diagnostics. Never carry a price across the age limit. An observed bar does not establish quote update age or depth. Final market status cannot reconstruct an intraday suspension.

Require at least **60 development, 20 validation and 30 test** usable labeled games, including at least ten development outcomes of each class and variation in market log odds. Determine test label *availability* from final binary metadata without reading the direction for analysis. For development fit, labels must have settled before the first possible validation observation (November 13 at 09:00 UTC). Validation labels must have settled before November 19 at 09:00 UTC. Do not refit on validation. Before fitting, report class and price coverage and precision scenarios for paired Brier-difference SD of 0.02 and 0.05. Anticipate wider uncertainty with temporal dependence. A failed coverage gate keeps test directions sealed.

Fit an unpenalized Bernoulli logistic regression with intercept and slope on market log odds by maximum likelihood. Stop if the fit fails or separates. Freeze coefficients and the development input digest locally before validation and test scoring. No hyperparameter search or threshold adjustment. Improvement is raw-market loss minus adjusted loss. Use mean paired Brier improvement as primary, log loss as secondary, and fixed probability bins [0,.2), [.2,.4), [.4,.6), [.6,.8), [.8,1]. Summarize sample size, mean forecast and observed fraction by bin. Estimate test uncertainty with 10,000 two-calendar-day moving-block bootstrap resamples, seeded 314159, keeping games together and preserving empty days. Report the exploratory percentile interval and distinguish the handful of time blocks from the number of games. Compare absolute corrections with spread and verified fee information where improvements warrant it.

A test improvement of at least 0.005 with an interval above zero and validation improvement in the same direction earns **PROMISING EXPLORATORY EVIDENCE**. An interval with upper endpoint below 0.005 earns **NO SUPPORT IN SAMPLE** for the prespecified effect. Inadequate eligible counts, unresolved class/fit issues or an interval crossing the relevant decision boundary earn **INSUFFICIENT PRECISION**. A failed acquisition or indefensible historical clock with no usable cohort earns **DATA BLOCKED**. The acquisition ceiling is 500 anonymous public attempts, no faster than one per second, and 100 MiB. Use A3.0's stricter 50 MiB byte limit in a separate E3 ledger. No automatic expansion.
