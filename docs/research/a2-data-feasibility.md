# A2 decision on genuine research data

**Decision, 25 November 2026.** A bounded, zero-cost study of calibration in the Good Judgment Project (GJP) experimental prediction markets is worth a data-quality audit. Two sampled binary questions link prices to final outcomes. The next uncertainty is whether enough *distinct events* have prices whose timing and interpretation survive a chronological holdout. Kalshi-specific calibration, cross-market valuation and economic performance remain untested. The [source register](a2-source-register.md) records what was actually inspected. The [A3 protocol](a3-calibration-protocol-draft.md) is a proposal for review.

## What the Predictions API documents

These are schema findings. No Kalshi data endpoint was called. The market reference examined embeds OpenAPI version **3.38.0** under the **v2** API path. The reviewed public market and trade operations declare no authentication requirement in their schemas. No project access or actual response was verified. Record both identifiers and the retrieval date in any licensed study. Recheck field units, status vocabulary and deprecated fields against the Predictions changelog. [Market schema](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market.md) · [API changelog](https://docs.kalshi.com/changelog)

| Research need | Documented representation and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Population and grouping | Series ticker identifies a recurring template, category and settlement sources. Event ticker links to series and may flag mutual exclusivity. Use series category because the event category is deprecated. Each market ticker belongs to an event. A series can span many independent outcomes, and separate event tickers can refer to one real-world outcome. |
| Rule and time | Use market side definitions, primary and secondary rules, strike type, bounds, settlement source and applicable contract terms. Distinguish creation, opening, trading close, expected/latest expiration, occurrence when available, and actual settlement. Early closing and changed terms can invalidate a reconstructed cutoff. A final schema snapshot does not establish what was known earlier. |
| Labels | Settled records can have a settlement timestamp, YES settlement value and result. Result admits yes, no, scalar and empty. Score only verified binary final payouts consistent with the rules and notional. Record pending, ambiguous, void or nonbinary cases separately. |
| Activity and liquidity | Market snapshots describe current best bids/asks, YES best size, last price, total/24h contract volume and open interest. Candles carry period volume and open interest. These can characterize activity or spread. Historical executable size, queue position, latency and full book evolution remain unverified. |
| Observations | Public trades identify ticker, trade ID, execution time (`created_time`), side prices and quantity. The live listing includes block trades by default and has a block flag. Verify archived fields before applying a uniform filter. The current book lists YES/NO bid levels with size, from which opposite-side asks follow for a standard binary contract. |

[Series](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-series.md) · [Event](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/events/get-event.md) · [Market](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market.md) · [Public trades](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-trades.md) · [Current order book](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market-orderbook)

For a licensed historical study, record a snapshot of the moving cutoff fields. Route markets and candles by **settlement time** (`market_settled_ts`), trades by **fill time** (`trades_created_ts`). A recently settled market can have old trades in the archive. Order and position cutoffs have independent meanings outside this study. Events and series remain discoverable, but live listings and nested event children omit archived markets. Combine live market and trade listings with `/historical/markets` and `/historical/trades`. Fetch archived candles by market ticker. Follow each listing's opaque cursor with identical filters until empty, detect repeats, and reconcile overlaps and gaps across the moving boundary. Deduplicate markets by ticker, trades by trade ID, candles by ticker, interval and end time. Quarantine conflicting versions and record both provenance and counts. Neither a successful page nor an empty interval proves complete historical coverage. [Historical tier guide](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/historical_data) · [Pagination](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/pagination)

Live and archived candle intervals are 1, 60 and 1,440 minutes, with a 5,000-period request limit. Their inclusive end is in Unix seconds. Require the entire interval and its availability to precede the research decision. Exclude synthetic previous-price anchors, partial bars and null sides. REST snapshots can lag, and candle extrema from two sides need not coincide. The archive guide lists no public historical depth replay. [Live candles](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market-candlesticks.md) · [Archived candles](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/historical/get-historical-market-candlesticks.md) · [Historical tier guide](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/historical_data)

| Quantity at a cutoff | Valid interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Last traded price | Most recent eligible YES execution at or before cutoff. Its age and trade type matter. A stale or privately negotiated print may poorly represent current beliefs. |
| Bid–ask midpoint | Mean of contemporaneous valid best YES bid and ask. Captures a consensus proxy. Requires a time-aligned two-sided quote and a bounded spread/age policy. |
| Executable side | Buying YES faces the ask, selling YES faces the bid, for available size at that moment. Costs, queueing and later price changes affect fills. A trade print proves someone transacted. |
| Candle open/close | First/last observation of the specified field in the interval. Separate trade OHLC from bid and ask OHLC. Side closes may justify a labeled proxy only after verifying synchronization and timing. |
| Settlement value | Realized payout after adjudication. It is a label and must never supply the historical probability. |

Use field-specific decimals. Current price strings in dollars can have four decimal places and contract quantities two. Integer-cent legacy values can lose sub-cent precision. Retain original units and market tick bands, distinguish missing from zero, and normalize against contract notional before treating a price as a probability. Authenticated limits use separate read/write token budgets, with most requests costing ten tokens. Public anonymous requests have separate limits. Bound any licensed sample, respect its assigned tier and back off on HTTP 429. ## Ranked research feasibility

Rank reflects the value of the next research question at the current budget. Cost is the expected data charge for the route shown. None of the four hypotheses has an estimated financial return.

| Rank | Hypothesis and status | Authorization and availability | Scientific test, complexity and unresolved assumptions | Recurring cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | H1 calibration — conditionally feasible in GJP. Kalshi blocked by authorization. | CC0 transaction and resolution data, two candidate binary links verified. | Moderate. Can a predeclared price predict outcome frequency at a reproducible horizon? Event count, timestamp zone, price timing and sample precision need an audit. | $0 GJP. Kalshi unquoted. |
| 2 | H4 independent forecasting — weather conditionally feasible, college features partly available. | NOAA operational forecasts and station outcomes have an open route. CBBD licenses private research and publishes free and paid tiers. | Moderate. Can a fixed forecast improve on seasonal climatology or a pregame team-strength baseline? Main risks are release timing, revisions and exact target definitions. | $0 weather and basic college data. Some historical college ratings $10/month. |
| 3 | H2 contract consistency — conditional for a manually verified subset. | GJP offers option and branch definitions. Adequate simultaneous price histories and exhaustive partitions unverified. Kalshi rules and prices need permission. | High adjudication effort per family. Test probability identities only for matching settlement states and attributable observations. Evidence for economic simulation is insufficient. | $0 GJP. Historical depth/rights unpriced. |
| 4 | H3 cross-market valuation — blocked for the proposed Kalshi comparison. | No licensed Kalshi side, no free historical Odds API prices, no independently verified pair of equivalent pre-cutoff observations. | High matching and timing effort. Independent source value could be tested by paired out-of-time loss on the same outcomes after removing vig and aligning rules. Overlap and selection remain unmeasured. | Historical odds from $30/month, plus unquoted Kalshi rights. |

**H1.** Count raw observations, eligible contracts and distinct underlying events separately. Repeated prints, tournament arms and complementary sides cannot inflate the independent sample. Require known category (an explicitly declared experimental cohort if no category taxonomy exists), binary outcome and both decision and observation times. Group chronological splits by event. Favorite–longshot shape, horizon effects and changing category mix are hypotheses for stratified diagnostics. Probabilities, outcomes and timing could support a scoring study even where liquidity and fee histories preclude economics. [Protocol](a3-calibration-protocol-draft.md)

**H2.** Write a truth table from versioned rules, named oracle, universe, units, time zone, observation window, strict/inclusive thresholds and void, tie, revision and fallback rules. Disjoint outcomes imply a probability sum at most one. Equality additionally requires exhaustiveness, including any “other” or cancellation state. For nested events the narrower probability cannot exceed the broader one. True complements sum to one, and logically equivalent predicates have equal probabilities. An exchange's mechanically derived opposite-side quote supplies no independent evidence. Require identical settlement states, rule validity at the cutoff and synchronized prices or uncertainty intervals. The GJP sample contains identically titled conditional branches with different dispositions. Exclude unresolved mappings. A discrepancy between stale prints or mids establishes neither simultaneous fills nor positive returns after fees. [Source register](a2-source-register.md)

**H3.** Construct a proposed crosswalk from league/tournament, season, participants, venue or neutral site, scheduled start known then, round, side and market type. Provider IDs are local. Review postponements, duplicates and ambiguous matches before examining forecast errors. De-vig all sides of a single bookmaker's market at the same snapshot. A model probability, a bookmaker probability and an exchange quote retain distinct provenance. Compare an external source or frozen combination to the market baseline by paired event-level scores.

| Sport | Specific evidence and reason a historical match may fail |
| --- | --- |
| NCAA men | CBBD lists game/provider lines from 2013 with variable coverage. Open and other game lines lack a per-quote timestamp in the documented schema. Odds API lists NCAAB snapshots since November 2020. FiveThirtyEight's sampled 2014 team-round advancement probabilities describe tournament survival. Matching a game winner requires a separate head-to-head forecast, overtime and neutral-site rules, and timestamped bookmaker coverage. |
| NBA | Odds API lists snapshots since June 2020. The sampled 2022 FiveThirtyEight game probabilities fall inside that calendar era and include both teams. Their date lacks issue time. Normalize home/away, postponements, playoffs and overtime. This is the strongest candidate for a later match audit. No matched game at a common cutoff was verified. |
| Tennis | Historical odds start separately for each tournament, e.g. French Open in September 2020 and Wimbledon in June 2021. The sampled 2016 FiveThirtyEight files concern round advancement. Draw changes, retirements, walkovers, tour, match format and rules on a ball being played can change eligibility or payout. No verified match-level common population. |

Odds API featured historical snapshots are spaced 10 minutes initially and five minutes from September 2022. Current headline pregame lines typically update every 60 seconds. CBBD generally updates game results within minutes of completion, while FiveThirtyEight sample issue times are date-only or absent. Historical bookmaker snapshots, opening/closing summaries and repeated same-day forecasts have different information content. Sportsbook, marquee-event and tournament selection may bias the common subset. Report unmatched events and exclusions by sport, provider and time. [Historical odds and sport starts](https://the-odds-api.com/historical-odds-data/) · [Odds update cadence](https://the-odds-api.com/sports-odds-data/update-intervals.html) · [CBBD lines](https://api.collegebasketballdata.com/api/lines) · [CBBD updates](https://www.collegebasketballdata.com/about) · [Sample results](a2-source-register.md)

**H4.** For weather, define one station, local-standard-time day, threshold, units, rounding and missing-observation rule. An archived operational HRRR cycle can supply a predictor only after its file was available. Compare it to climatology estimated from earlier outcomes. A later quality-controlled daily maximum can label that day, but cannot be used as a same-day historical input. Separately validate station-to-contract oracle alignment for any exchange claim. For college basketball, use completed prior games available before scheduled tip, preserve season/team identifiers, and treat season-end aggregates, corrected box scores and retrospective ratings as potential leakage. Daily snapshot methodology and proof of publication time determine which features qualify. No model was trained. [NOAA and college source evidence](a2-source-register.md)

## Recommended operator decision

Approve, as a separate assignment, a bounded GJP audit of one experimental arm and its question records. Review the A3 protocol and set a byte/field cap and precision target before expanding the sample. Resolve branch/side mapping, point-in-time schedule, timezone, price sequence and price availability, then report event-grouped coverage by period. If a gate fails, narrow the claim or stop. A Kalshi-specific assignment separately requires written scope and price from the provider. The operator may use the unsent draft above. A2 spent $0, used no exchange credentials and acquired no Kalshi market records.
