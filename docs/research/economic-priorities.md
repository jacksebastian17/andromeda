# Economic research priorities

The immediate question is which observable patterns could survive costs, adverse selection and event-level uncertainty. These experiments are ordered by the evidence available to start them. No expected profit ranking is supported. Before making an economic claim, identify the fee schedule applicable to the series and date, the sides and sizes available at the decision, spread, timing and uncertainty. Record later fee revisions separately. [Official fee schedule](https://kalshi.com/fee-schedule)

| Question | Economic rationale and required evidence | Main failure modes and costs | Smallest test and falsification rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Do complementary NBA contracts agree? | The two mutually exclusive team-win probabilities should sum to one under identical final rules. A disagreement can identify a pricing or measurement problem with no outcome fitting. | Asynchronous trades or bar ends, mismatched void rules, different tick grids, bid–ask spread, fees and insufficient displayed size. | On development games only, align both YES bid/ask intervals and compare one with the complement of the other. Report overlap and signed separation by game. Drop the economic interpretation if intervals overlap after costs or temporal alignment is unverified. |
| Are market probabilities calibrated? | A stable correction could improve probability estimates. Require predeclared receipt-time prices, binary labels, independent games and a later untouched cohort. | Favorite/longshot selection, schedule changes, stale last trades, regime change, and a score improvement smaller than spread and fees. | First count usable scheduled horizons and timely labels prospectively. If coverage and precision become adequate, compare the raw market baseline with logistic recalibration by event-weighted paired Brier loss. Reject a repeatable improvement claim when a sufficiently precise independent interval rules out the predeclared useful effect. |
| Do independent sources add information? | An external estimate could explain deviations beyond the Kalshi-only baseline. Require separately permitted as-of snapshots, matching games/sides, overtime and cancellation rules, and two-sided odds to remove vig. | Incorrect joins, use of closing lines at an earlier cutoff, subscription cost, correlated sources and bookmaker margin. | On a small, lawfully available window, audit identity, timestamps and paired coverage before scoring. Stop if a common predecision population cannot be established. Later test incremental loss on the same games. |
| Is a specialized model worth maintaining? | Domain features available before market repricing might add independent predictive information. Require as-published features, exact label definitions and a low-cost baseline. | Revised statistics, injury-report timing, small samples, collinearity with the market, feature licensing and ongoing maintenance. | Test one dated feature family against the market and a simple domain baseline on chronological development folds. Drop it if vintages cannot be reconstructed or credible incremental accuracy disappears out of time. |
| Are apparent microstructure effects observable? | Spread, quote staleness and same-batch consistency may explain apparent advantages or constrain capacity. Require receipts, displayed sizes and enough distinct games. | Five-minute aliasing, provider caches, lost queue priority, latency, unobserved depth, adverse selection and fees. | Stratify snapshot coverage and quoted spread by time to the reported start. Repeat the analysis using bid/ask intervals instead of last trades. Drop an execution interpretation if it needs between-poll price paths, simultaneous fills or unavailable sizes. |

## Next bounded experiment

Start with the **A3.0 NBA development partition** while prospective collection runs. A feature-only audit found 14 development rows, 13 proxy-eligible rows across seven games and six eligible two-contract pairs. All six pairs share a research cutoff and have two-sided candle closes. **Only one pair shares a candle end.** Identical decision cutoffs alone would produce five asynchronous comparisons. No validation or holdout file, directional label or performance metric was used for this audit. [A3.0 feasibility and definitions](a3-0-campaign.md)

Use those six preidentified development pairs as a fixed whitelist. From their already archived individual candle responses, count common completed minute ends inside the first-hour window. For aligned minutes compare the two sides' quote intervals and last-trade deviations, then summarize once per game with counts for missing intervals and empty markets. Keep even a same-minute candle comparison explicitly retrospective because side updates can occur at different times. Report whether any apparent discrepancy survives interval overlap, plausible timing sensitivity and applicable fees. If coverage collapses or only trade-price differences remain, stop that line of inference. This exercise needs no new acquisition, outcome labels, strategy, simulator or dashboard work. A positive diagnostic would justify checking **fresh same-response** prospective quotes; it would still leave execution and profitability unproven.
