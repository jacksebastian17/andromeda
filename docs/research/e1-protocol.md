# E1 frozen analysis protocol

Use the existing A3.0 NBA development partition. Freeze its six previously identified pairs before opening candle bodies. Select only whole groups with two proxy-eligible NBA contracts from the direction-free development features. Pin the membership and feature hash in a private E1 whitelist. An inconsistent input or unexpected pair count stops the run. Keep the archive and partition files read-only. Read exact-event and candle responses for the whitelist only, with successful response hashes verified. Use market rule and identity fields without reading directions or normalized labels.

Use the two verified $1 YES contracts on the same full-game winner predicate and matching rule conditions. Compare the official winner case conditional on ordinary binary resolution. Separately check the archived cancellation and postponement language where supplied. If an exception can produce fair-price settlement, the two-payout sum is conditional. Shared event metadata and archived rules cannot verify the rules that were displayed at the time.

Use the shared first-hour A3.0 window. Build the UTC minute-end grid with a full preceding minute after opening and a 60-second lag before the common cutoff. Measure missing or unusable observations against that grid. Drop conflicting duplicate bars. A valid quote interval has exact decimal-dollar closes with `0 < bid ≤ ask < 1`. Endpoints may signify empty books. A trade close additionally requires positive in-minute volume and a price within notional. A previous-price anchor supplies no fresh trade. Use no interpolation or carry-forward. Report the fraction of comparable quotes on minutes with trades on both sides as an activity sensitivity, with consecutive recorded quotes unchanged as a separate staleness proxy. Neither identifies actual quote age.

1. Compare the two latest individually eligible A3.0 closes per game as an explicitly unaligned baseline. Report nonzero trade sums minus one dollar, bid–ask incompatibility, and the spread of timestamp gaps. On the same set of games also compare the latest common quote and latest common trade minute where present. Any change across this comparison includes the effect of reselecting a minute.
2. At each common end compare last trades when both are available and compare the midpoint sum on the same denominator as the quote intervals. Quotes are compatible exactly when one dollar lies in the closed interval from the sum of bids to the sum of asks. Record low-ask and high-bid cases separately, and compute mean absolute deviations and frequency by game, then jointly as aggregate counts and game-weighted means. Minutes are repeated observations. Do not infer an exchange-wide rate or assume games sharing teams are statistically independent.
3. Recompute at minus and plus one minute on the second ticker in sorted order using exact observed bars and no fill-in. Those comparisons measure sensitivity to a deliberate mismatch. Widen each aligned interval to the lowest bid and highest ask printed within its bar when both sides provide valid ranges enclosing their closes. Count how many aligned discrepancies survive that envelope. Recount on both-traded minutes and under 0- and 120-second lag alternatives. The printed ranges and lag scenarios give no bound on unobserved quote updates or dissemination.
4. On displayed closes only, compare each strict gross gap to the sum of two one-contract fees at the corresponding prices. Run zero, 3.5%, 7% and 14% coefficients on `p(1-p)`, rounded up separately to cents for each contract as a pre-July sensitivity convention. These are scenarios unless May 2026 series and event applicability can be independently verified. Use strict positivity, including when cost equals gap. Report counts and magnitude in cents per $1 of ordinary settlement as measurement diagnostics.

Classify **NO SUPPORT IN SAMPLE** if at least four games and 30 aligned quote minutes are available and no gross gap survives, or all gross gaps disappear under verified applicable costs. Classify **PROMISING DIAGNOSTIC — requires prospective confirmation** only if at least two games retain a same-direction gap under the within-minute envelope and the 7% fee scenario on the corresponding aligned closes, on minutes with trades in both contracts. Use **INCONCLUSIVE — insufficient or ambiguous evidence** otherwise. These are descriptive decision rules. A surviving discrepancy would justify a small same-response receipt-time quote check with positive reported size and rule and fee checks. Historical candles cannot establish simultaneous executable depth, fills or returns.
